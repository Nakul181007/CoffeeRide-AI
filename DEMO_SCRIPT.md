# CoffeeRide AI — 60-90 Second Hackathon Presentation Script

**Speaker / Presenter Script:**

> "Imagine you're a coffee company preparing to expand into a new city..."
> 
> "...Traditionally, picking a new store location means spending tens of thousands of dollars on manual foot-traffic surveys and static demographic reports. But what if you could tap directly into dynamic, real-time public mobility patterns?
> 
> Meet **CoffeeRide AI**. 
> 
> Here on our landing page, we enter our target market—**New York City**—and select our store concept: a **Cyclist-focused coffee shop**. 
> 
> When we click **Analyze Market**, CoffeeRide AI launches an autonomous agentic workflow. Step-by-step, the agent connects to mobility datasets, inspects cycling unlock activity, analyzes trip origins and destinations across 1.5 thousand stations, and calculates our Cyclist Opportunity Score.
> 
> In seconds, Gemini delivers our **Expansion Intelligence Dashboard**. 
> 
> Our top candidate is **Downtown Brooklyn**, scoring an impressive **91 out of 100**. The dashboard breaks down our top 5 candidate corridors—including Williamsburg and Lower Manhattan—backed by key mobility metrics like morning trip-start density and peak commuter traffic.
> 
> Gemini provides natural-language AI reasoning explaining *why* Downtown Brooklyn wins: heavy commuter flow across the Brooklyn Bridge corridor paired with high morning docking traffic creates an ideal environment for specialty coffee conversion.
> 
> In production, CoffeeRide AI is designed to query public BigQuery datasets directly through the BigQuery MCP Server. 
> 
> **CoffeeRide AI turns public mobility data into an actionable expansion decision.** Thank you!"
